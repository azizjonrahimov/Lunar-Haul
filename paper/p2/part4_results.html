5.1 The road layer on the base's roads

At the default single-lane share, {{P2-SINGLE-KM-DEFAULT}} of the {{P2-ROUTE-KM}} of road is one lane wide, in segments split by bays every kilometre. Over a simulated year at today's fleet, vehicles in the ATC mode made {{P2-ATC-X1-TRAVERSALS}} traversals of those segments, met {{P2-ATC-X1-CONFLICTS}} conflicts, and waited {{P2-ATC-X1-BAY-WAIT-H}} at bays in all, across the fleet. They waited {{P2-ATC-X1-APRON-WAIT-H}} for an apron slot and drove {{P2-ATC-X1-EVA-SLOW-H}} at crew speed through the habitat zone during EVAs. Landings closed a pad {{P2-ATC-X1-STANDDOWNS}} times a year, as in [41]. The bay wait fell on the owners unequally: {{P2-OWNER-A-BAY-WAIT-X1}} for owner A, {{P2-OWNER-B-BAY-WAIT-X1}} for owner B and {{P2-OWNER-C-BAY-WAIT-X1}} for owner C. No run in any cell of E43 recorded a deadlock ({{P2-DEADLOCKS-TOTAL-N}} in {{P2-N-RUNS-E43}} runs).
