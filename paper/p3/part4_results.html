5. Results

The results come in the paper's two halves. The design and its layers are in Sections 5.1 to 5.4 and 5.8 to 5.13; the market is in Sections 5.5 to 5.7. Sections 5.1 to 5.4 give E48: the processor time of the safety check and the estimate of the on-robot data against the flight processor (H10), the radio traffic and the ledger, the link budget per class, and failover. Sections 5.5 to 5.7 give E46: the four payment rules, individual rationality per owner, and manipulation with audits (H5). Sections 5.8 to 5.13 give the experiments of the November 2026 revision: position error by layer (E50 and E50b, H9′), clocks (E51, H11), the shared map (E52, H12), the protocol's traffic and its authentication (E53 and E55, H13), store-and-forward with the lost-link rule (E54, H14), and two checks on real data; Tables 10 to 15 hold their values. Section 5.14 lists what did not work and what was not run. Table 6 holds every sizing value, Table 7 the link budget, Table 8 every cell mean of E46 and Table 9 every owner's Shapley value in every coalition against working alone; the text describes them in that order. Intervals are 95 % bootstrap intervals over seeds unless stated. Nothing in this section is interpreted; Section 6 does that.

5.14 What did not work, and what was not run

No result is awaited. H9′'s second clause was not evaluated under its readings at eight times the fleet (5.8). An internal referee round changed what several results of the revision are called, without changing a number that a results file holds: Sections 4.1 and 4.6 list them. {{P3-CHECKS6-OUTCOME-SENTENCE}} Two results of the revision were corrected before this paper used them. The atomic-clock rows of E51 first restarted the clock's ageing at every resynchronisation; the model now lets it accumulate, the rows of Table 11 are the corrected ones, and the crystal's rows and H11 did not change. The first table of E54 counted ledger bundles that were still on their way at the end of the 31-day window as lost; the ledger was simulated again over 61 days, and Section 5.12 quotes that run only. {{P3-E46-STATUS-SENTENCE}} {{P3-E48-STATUS-SENTENCE}} {{P3-FAILED-CHECKS-SENTENCE}} {{P3-ID-FIX-SENTENCE}} The link budget of the first version of this paper used free-space loss at the worst hop, and its link verdict compared the target with the traffic at the sources, without authentication or relaying; both were corrected on {{P3-E48-REVISION-DATE}} (Sections 4.6 and 7), and Sections 5.1 to 5.3 give the corrected values. Nothing else is withheld: every cell that ran is in Table 8 and every measurement made is in Tables 6 and 7.
