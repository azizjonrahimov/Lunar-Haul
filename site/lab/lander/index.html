Lander lab: the last 500 metres

Watch a lander fly its last 500 m: from the start of the final approach (NASA's SPLICE reference point, 430 m up and 250 m out) to touchdown. It runs in your browser with the same equations as the Python model. Let a trained neural network or the classical guidance law fly it, watch the score the network was trained on, change the reward rules, and compare both over 500 landings with random starting errors.

Masses are at the start of the final approach. The 60 t lander starts 400 m out at 25 m/s, because it cannot stop within 250 m. Each start number gives a different, repeatable set of starting errors and noise.
