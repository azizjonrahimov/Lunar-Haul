Labs

Two labs that run the simulation's own models in your browser, on real Moon terrain (NASA LOLA). Each one shows every constant with its flag and source.

Lander lab: the last 500 metres

A lander flies from the start of the final approach to touchdown at pad A, with the same equations as the Python model.

  • Replay recorded descents in 3D: a trained neural network or the classical guidance law at the controls.
  • Change the reward rules and watch the score the network was trained on.
  • Compare both over 500 landings with random starting errors.

Rover lab: drive a leg of the base

Pick two sites, a driver and the lighting, then drive the route with the model the logistics simulation takes its driving times from.

  • Slope limits, wheel slip, glare from the low sun, shadows and energy use.
  • Drive it yourself and feel what the Earth–Moon signal delay does.
  • Race the built-in expert against the neural-network driver on the same leg.

The simulator runs the logistics model itself: a whole year of the base, in your browser.
