What the driver sees and does

A driving policy gets a 32 × 32 grid around the rover, 5 m per cell, rotated with the rover (forward is up; the outlined cell is the rover), plus five numbers. It answers with two: a steering rate and a throttle. These are the exact inputs the learned driver will get.
