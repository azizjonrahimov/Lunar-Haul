Earth's sky, year by year

From the south pole Earth never climbs more than a few degrees, and ridges hide it much of the time. One short stretch of the regolith haul from the oxygen plant (ISRU) to the construction site (CONSTR) sees Earth only when Earth stands high and a little east of grid north. Which way Earth leans when it is high changes from year to year, and in 2028 it leans the wrong way.
