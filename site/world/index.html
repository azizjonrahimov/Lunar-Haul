The replay is one year (seed 3); the numbers are the mean of 20 years with the same settings, with their 95 % interval. Ring colours under the rovers: cyan driving, green loading, grey parked for the night, amber holding outside a landing keep-out, purple waiting for Earth, red recovering from a fault. Models are representative; icons are drawn larger than scale.

Runs from the browser simulator and trace files open in the trace viewer, which also has a 2D plan view.
